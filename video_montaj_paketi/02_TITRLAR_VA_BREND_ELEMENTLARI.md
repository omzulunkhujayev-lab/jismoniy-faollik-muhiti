## 🔤 Tavsiya Etiladigan Shriftlar (Fonts)

Professional, o'qilishi oson va barcha tillarni (O'zbek, Rus, Ingliz) to'liq qo'llab-quvvatlovchi zamonaviy bepul Google Shriftlari:

1. **Inter** (Asosiy sarlavhalar va titrlar uchun):
   - Og'irliklar: `Bold (700)`, `ExtraBold (800)`, `SemiBold (600)`
   - Yuklab olish: [Google Fonts — Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter)
2. **Montserrat** (Raqamlar, statistika va qadamlar hisobi uchun):
   - Og'irliklar: `Black (900)`, `Bold (700)`
   - Yuklab olish: [Google Fonts — Montserrat](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Montserrat)
3. **Poppins** (Qo'shimcha izohlar va subtitrlar uchun):
   - Og'irliklar: `Medium (500)`, `Regular (400)`
   - Yuklab olish: [Google Fonts — Poppins](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins)

## 📐 Video O'lchamlari va Kadr Tezligi (Resolution & FPS)

### A. Gorizontal Format (16:9) — Dissertatsiya Himoyasi va YouTube:
- **Kenglik x Balandlik:** `3840 x 2160` (4K UHD) yoki `1920 x 1080` (Full HD)
- **Kadr tezligi (FPS):** `60 fps` (animatsiyalar o'ta silliq chiqishi uchun) yoki `30 fps`
- **Piksel nisbati:** Square Pixels (1.0)

### B. Vertikal Format (9:16) — Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok:
- **Kenglik x Balandlik:** `1080 x 1920` (Full HD Vertical)
- **Kadr tezligi (FPS):** `60 fps`
- **Xavfsiz zona (Safe Zone):** Yuqoridan 150px va pastdan 250px ochiq qoldiring (Instagram tugmalari va sarlavhalar ustiga tushib qolmasligi uchun).
